A haiku about debugging, in the classic 5-7-5 shape — seventeen syllables
about the loneliest hour of the craft.

## The haiku

```
Cursor blinks at three
the log says nothing is wrong
the bug was my own
```

## Syllable count (17 total)

| Line | Words | Syllables |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cursor (2) blinks (1) at (1) three (1) | **5** |
| 2 | the (1) log (1) says (1) nothing (2) is (1) wrong (1) | **7** |
| 3 | the (1) bug (1) was (1) my (1) own (1) | **5** |

The concrete image at the centre is a cursor blinking at 3 a.m. beside a
log that insists nothing is wrong — and the last line turns the poem: the
bug was never in the machine, it was mine all along.
